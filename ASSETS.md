# Visual asset provenance

The reference is the user-supplied `FSGM_Advisory_Trifold_Print_Ready.pdf`. Its navy, teal, warm-white, and gold palette guides this website.

- `public/images/community-river.webp`: original image generated for this website, depicting an illustrative wooded river valley and riverside community. It does not identify a specific place or client. The generated source remains outside the repository; this WebP is optimized for delivery without changing the composition.
- `public/images/community-town.png`, `pennsylvania-capitol.png`, `financial-planning.png`, and `collaboration.png`: embedded images extracted unchanged from the supplied brochure. They are reference/community imagery, not claims about FSGM staff, clients, offices, or government affiliation. Original image rights are not independently asserted by this project.
- `public/brandmark.svg` and `public/favicon.svg`: original vector FSGM lettermarks, adapted to the brochure palette.
- Fonts: self-hosted DM Sans and Source Serif 4, distributed with their included open font licenses through Fontsource packages.

No third-party image URL is hotlinked. The hero has explicit dimensions and high fetch priority; supporting images load lazily. The brochure itself is not published by this change.
